GROOVE FROM EMPTY SPACE · EIGHT VOICES · SELF-BUILDING SEQUENCES

TOMRUM is Swedish for "empty space". It is a drum and groove synth that builds its music from what is not played. Press play and it is already running: eight voices, a sequence written by the rulebook of a school, timing that sits exactly wrong, and sound that slowly rots over the night.

01Welcome

Generative sequencers always fill in too much. TOMRUM takes away.

The German and Romanian minimal schools of the late 90s and 2000s worked like engineers, not songwriters: cut away everything that is not needed, leave a naked, pulsing skeleton, and let the timing sit a few milliseconds off so that the skeleton swings. TOMRUM is built around that idea. Every pattern comes from a rulebook, every hit has its own friction behind the grid, and a MINUS control removes hits until the groove is made of the gaps.

  • Eight voices synthesised inside: kick, sub bass, click, hat, wood block, scrape, chord stab and a ghost that only plays in the silence.
  • Eight schools, each one a rulebook for rhythm, off-grid feel and sound.
  • GENETIK builds a new groove and sound from the rulebook. Never free chaos.
  • PARASITE: drop any drum loop on the panel and TOMRUM steals how crooked it sits.
  • ROT: nothing sounds the same for long. Over about seven minutes the ceiling sinks and the stereo narrows.
  • 800 presets, one hundred per school.

02Install

macOS: unzip TOMRUM-macOS.zip from your Downloads folder, open Terminal and run:

bash ~/Downloads/TOMRUM/installera.command

Quit your DAW completely and start it again. TOMRUM shows up among the instruments. Requires macOS 11 or newer, Intel or Apple Silicon.

Windows: unzip TOMRUM-Windows.zip and copy TOMRUM.vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3.

Put TOMRUM on an instrument track. That is all. It needs no notes in: it plays by itself and follows the DAW's tempo and play/stop.

03Your first minute

  1. Press play in your DAW. No DAW? The standalone app starts by itself; otherwise press SPELA.
  2. Pick a SKOLA. Changing school runs GENETIK at once.
  3. Hit GENETIK until something grabs you. Or step through presets with the arrows.
  4. F (freeze) the lanes you love. Hit GENETIK again: only the others change.
  5. Click a lane's name to re-roll only that lane. LJUD keeps the groove and rolls a new sound.
  6. Turn MINUS up and listen to the groove get better as it gets emptier.
  7. Went too far? ÅNGRA goes back, up to 40 steps.

The panel

Left: the eight lanes. Each shows its cycle as cells: a full amber cell is a hit, a grey dash is a step where the rulebook allows a hit but none was chosen. A lane that is not 16 steps long shows its length at the end of the cycle, so you can see the polyrhythm. In the middle: the void scope, one ring per lane, kick innermost. The glow in the centre grows when nothing is playing: the more silence, the more it lights up. The red arc round the edge is how far ROT has gone.

04The eight schools

A school is a rulebook. For every lane it holds up to three pattern variants written as probabilities per step, a Markov rule for what follows a hit, a friction range in milliseconds, how many hits are allowed per cycle, and whether the lane may hit together with the kick. It also holds the sound: kick pitch and length, hat colour, wood pitch, chord, ceiling, dub, pump, ring modulation, bit crushing, crackle and noise bed. GENETIK only ever chooses inside those limits.

SchoolCharacter
UNDER VATTENEverything drowned: a 2 kHz ceiling, dub, pump against an endless four. Like banging your head on a concrete wall under water. 124 BPM.

06GENETIK, freeze and presets

GENETIK builds a new genome: patterns for every lane that is not frozen, plus a new sound, all from the school's rulebook. The knobs MINUS, SVÄNG, DUB and PUMP jump to the genome's values, so you can hear where the school wants to be and then push it.

Clicking a lane's name runs GENETIK on that lane only. LJUD rolls a new sound and keeps the groove. Everything is undoable with ÅNGRA.

Presets: 800, one hundred per school. Each preset is a fixed seed, so it always comes back exactly the same. Frozen lanes are left alone when you change preset, so you can keep a kick and bass you love and browse everything on top of it. Your own rolled grooves are saved inside the DAW project, note for note.

Everything is calculated from the song position: rewind to bar 33 and you hear exactly what played at bar 33 last time. Loops and exports stay stable.

07Time: SWING, FRICTION, PARASITE

SVÄNG drags the odd 16ths, inside the school's own range.

FRIKTION gives every lane its own lateness: the school sets a range per lane (from a couple of milliseconds up to about 20) and every hit picks a fixed spot inside it. The kick never drags. 0 % is straight, 200 % is twice the school's friction.

PARASIT lays an off-grid template over the 16ths. Every school has its own. Or drop a drum loop (WAV, AIFF or FLAC) on the parasite box, or click it to pick a file: TOMRUM finds the hits, works out how many bars the loop is at your tempo, and measures how early or late every 16th sits. That crookedness is then laid onto TOMRUM's own hits. A hand drum played on a saucepan works beautifully. GLÖM LOOPEN goes back to the school's template.

The parasite box shows the template as 16 bars: down is late, up is early, the beats are white.

08Space: MINUS, DUB, PUMP, CEILING, ROT

MINUS removes hits. Weak steps go first, the kick never. The same hit always disappears in the same place, so the space becomes part of the pattern instead of random dropouts.

DUB is the chance that a single hit is thrown down into the basement echo. The rest stays dry, so one hit in four suddenly echoes away without muddying the mix.

PUMP lets the kick press everything else down. TAK (ceiling) is the low-pass above everything, 100 % is the school's ceiling.

RUTTNA (rot): from the moment you press play the ceiling sinks and the stereo width narrows, a little every bar, over about seven minutes. After a long set the knife-sharp loop has rotted down to a dull pulse in the basement without a single automation curve. Stop and play again to start fresh. At 0 % nothing rots.

VARIATION turns weak steps every fourth bar, always inside the rulebook, so nothing loops exactly the same.

09MIDI out and troubleshooting

Every hit is also sent as a MIDI note on channel 1 (see the table in chapter 05), so TOMRUM can double its groove on another drum machine or synth placed after it.

  • Nothing happens: is the DAW playing? Without a DAW, press SPELA.
  • macOS says the plugin is damaged: run installera.command again. It removes the quarantine flag.
  • The loop was not accepted: it needs at least six clear hits and should be a loop of 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars. Set your DAW tempo near the loop's tempo first.
  • It sounds too dull after a while: that is ROT doing its job. Turn RUTTNA down, or stop and play again.
  • Changing SKOLA by automation did nothing: automation only selects the school. Press GENETIK (or pick it on the panel) to build from it.
